For Aruni · with gratitude

The End.

Aruni,

I don't really know how to put everything into a few words, but I wanted to leave you with something honest.

Thank you. For the time, the memories, the laughter, the little moments that probably seemed ordinary at the time but mean a lot to me now. The Bhopal night walk, Tao Bao, and the beginning of us on the 19th and 20th of March — I'll always be grateful that I got to experience those moments with you.

I'm sorry for the times I hurt you, for the times I went quiet instead of explaining what was happening in my head, and for the moments when my actions didn't make you feel as valued as you deserved to feel. I know that saying sorry doesn't undo any of it. I just want you to know that I recognise it, and that I genuinely regret it.

Whatever happens from here, I won't pretend that none of it mattered. It did. You did. I'm grateful that our paths crossed, and I'll carry the good parts with me.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope you find reasons to smile, feel understood, and feel loved — always.

Thank you for everything.

Some chapters stay with us.
